Our yearly tradition

The Thomas Family Fundraiser

Every year our whole family picks one cause that’s close to our hearts, and we raise as much as we can for it by December 31.

How it works

  1. We choose together. Everyone in the family has a say, so the cause changes from year to year.
  2. You give however is easiest. Use our GoFundMe, or give straight to the charity. Either way, it all goes to the cause.
  3. We wrap up on December 31. Whatever we raise through the GoFundMe goes to the charity.
